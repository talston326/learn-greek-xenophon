## Source and language treatment

Readings explicitly identify adaptation, source passages, and changes to tense or sentence construction. Adapted dialogue and connective scenes are distinguished from historical reports. Epyaxa's contribution is reported with the source's uncertainty; her motives are not invented. Cunaxa distinguishes a local Greek victory from Cyrus's death. The seizure of the generals is distinguished from the killing of captains outside and the leaders' later fates. Xenophon becomes one of several replacement commanders.

Grammar follows the approved progression: third-declension and pronoun work in 13–16; present participles and irregular nouns in 17–18; futures in 19–20; second, first, and liquid aorists in 21–24. Forms outside the formal sequence receive reading support. Every grammar section has explanation, paradigms or construction tables, worked examples, and practice.

Detailed source checks and peer-review notes: [13–16](module-2-authoring-13-16.md), [17–20](module-2-authoring-17-20.md), [21–24](module-2-authoring-21-24.md). Three authoring agents used GPT-6.1 Sol with High reasoning, followed by independent cross-review and root integration.
