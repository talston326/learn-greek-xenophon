About this reading

This reader covers the complete 34-section work. The Greek follows E. C. Marchant's 1921 edition at Perseus. The English for §§11–34 draws on O. J. Todd's 1923 translation, with source footnotes omitted and §§22–23 edited to align with the Greek section break; §§1–10 retain course draft English. New word forms and morphology use provisional annotations from the Open Greek Corpus (CC BY-SA 4.0). Short lexical glosses draw on Perseus and Logeion. The phrase cues, syntax links, and reading-order arrangements are draft study aids for instructor review. The banner is an interpretive reconstruction of a court setting in the Agora; the precise trial venue is uncertain.
