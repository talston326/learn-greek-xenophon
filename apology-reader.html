About this reading

This preview covers §§1–9 and the opening of §10, ending at καὶ τοὺς νέους διαφθείροι, where the source sentence continues. The complete work has 34 numbered sections. The Greek follows E. C. Marchant's 1921 edition at Perseus. The new §§2–10 word forms use provisional, machine assisted annotations from the Open Greek Corpus annotations (CC BY-SA 4.0). Course glosses, translation, and syntax links are drafts for instructor review. The banner is an interpretive reconstruction of a court setting in the Agora; the precise trial venue is uncertain.
